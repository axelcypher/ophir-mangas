# The edition fills in its volumes; there are no line presets

Ophir Figures keeps presets per product line that fill in a figure's fields. In Ophir Mangas the edition already holds what all its volumes share (publisher, language, format, list price), so a new volume takes those from its edition and starts at the next free number, and "Nächster Band" creates it from the previous one. Line presets were left out on purpose (decided with the user on 2026-11-07); publisher-wide defaults such as one price for every Carlsen paperback were offered and declined.
